# ⚔️ Hoi4-mod: Prague vs Benešov

> A custom **Hearts of Iron IV** alternate-history mod focused on the conflict between **Prague** and **Benešov**.

## 🌍 About Hearts of Iron IV

[Hearts of Iron IV](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/heart-of-iron-iv/about) is a grand-strategy game set during the turbulent years surrounding the Second World War. Players guide a nation through diplomacy, politics, industry, research, military planning, and warfare while shaping their own version of history.

The game is especially well suited to alternate-history storytelling. Through national focuses, events, decisions, custom countries, technologies, and strategic choices, a familiar world can be transformed into something entirely new.

## 🛠️ About This Mod

This project is a fan-made modification for Hearts of Iron IV. We are creating a custom scenario centered on **Prague vs Benešov**, with its own setting, conflicts, and alternate-history direction.

The mod may include:

- Custom national focuses and political paths
- New events and decisions
- Alternate-history lore and storytelling
- Unique countries, characters, and leaders
- Custom military, diplomatic, and economic gameplay
- New map, state, or province content as development progresses

## 🧩 Modding Hearts of Iron IV

HoI4 mods are built by editing the game's data files, usually written in a structured text format. Modders can change nearly every part of the experience, including gameplay rules, country history, national focuses, event chains, localisation, graphics, sounds, and more.

Creating a mod is a collaborative process involving game design, writing, coding, testing, balancing, and artwork. This repository will be used to organize the development of the Prague vs Benešov project and track its progress over time.

## 🚧 Development Status

This mod is currently in development. Features, lore, and gameplay systems may change as the project grows.

## 📜 Project Goals

1. Create an engaging alternate-history setting.
2. Make Prague and Benešov feel distinct and interesting to play.
